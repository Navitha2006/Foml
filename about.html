🏨 About InnoStay

InnoStay – Innovative Stay Price Predictor – is an AI-powered platform that helps travelers find affordable hotel stays with intelligent price forecasting. Our system predicts hotel room rates based on real-world trends, seasonal factors, and weekend patterns. The project was built as part of the Foundation of AI initiative by our passionate team of developers aiming to combine innovation, data science, and hospitality. With a focus on user experience, privacy, and transparency, InnoStay transforms how travelers plan their perfect stay.
